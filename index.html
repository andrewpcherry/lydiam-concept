Questions, answered.

Who can open an account?

Companies, family offices, institutions and private clients. Lydiam does not advertise or offer services to people in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Final eligibility is decided by Lydiam's compliance team.

What is a named IBAN?

An account number in your own company or personal name, so the people paying you see you rather than a pooled account. Lydiam shows named accounts in USD, EUR, GBP and JPY.

How much does it cost?

Every fee will be listed on the pricing page, with a clear markup over the mid-market rate. Final pricing is set by Lydiam and depends on volume.

How do I know an email is really from you?

Check for your private safety phrase, and use "Verify a Lydiam contact" above. We only email from lydiamgroup.com.

Can I fix a rate for a future payment?

Yes. Spot quotes are held while you confirm, limit orders convert automatically at your target, and forwards fix a rate for a future date.
