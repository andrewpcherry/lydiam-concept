Money, as an API.

Quotes, conversions, payouts and named accounts for your own customers, with signed webhooks for every change. Sandbox access on request.

  • QuotesLive rates with a hold window, so your customer sees exactly what they'll pay.
  • PayoutsSend to bank accounts in the currencies Lydiam supports, single or in bulk.
  • Named accountsIssue accounts in your customers' own names, one per customer or entity.
  • WebhooksSigned events for every state change, from quote to settlement.
  • Built for agentsConnect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor over MCP, with approvals kept in human hands.
Illustrative. Final API reference to be published by Lydiam.
